What we do

From circuit to
working prototype.

Practical engineering support for early-stage ideas, product teams and academic projects. Scope can start small and grow with your requirements.

01 / PCB↗

Custom PCB design

2-layer and 4-layer PCB layouts, schematics, component selection, footprints, routing, design reviews and fabrication-ready files.

02 / EMBEDDED⌘

Embedded systems & firmware

Microcontroller firmware, sensor integration, communication protocols, driver development, debugging and board bring-up.

03 / IoT⌁

IoT product development

Connected devices using Wi-Fi, BLE and suitable wired interfaces, with cloud or dashboard integration where required.

04 / PROTOTYPE＋

Electronics prototyping

Proof-of-concept builds, module integration, quick test fixtures, engineering validation and iterative improvements.

05 / INSTITUTIONS▦

College & institutional projects

Affordable, guided electronics project development for students, labs and faculty — including documentation and demonstrations.

06 / PRODUCT◈

Product engineering support

Technical feasibility, BOM planning, component sourcing guidance, enclosure-aware board design and prototype iteration.

07 / SENSORS⌁

Sensor & data acquisition systems

Analog front ends, sensor conditioning, data logging and acquisition prototypes for research and engineering applications.

08 / DESIGN REVIEW◎

PCB review & troubleshooting

Review existing designs, help diagnose hardware issues, and identify practical fixes before the next prototype run.

09 / LEARNING↗

Technical documentation

Readable schematics, connection guides, firmware notes, test procedures and project reports matched to the work.

How we work

Small steps.
Tested hardware.

No bloated process. We agree on the goal, make the scope clear, and build in iterations so problems surface early.

Discuss your project ↗
01

Understand the brief

We discuss the use case, required features, budget, deadline and what a successful prototype should prove.

02

Define scope & estimate

You receive a practical plan covering deliverables, assumptions, milestones and estimated costs before work begins.

03

Design & develop

We work through schematics, PCB layout, firmware and integration in reviewable stages.

04

Test, document, iterate

We verify the agreed functionality, share findings and deliver the files and documentation defined in the project scope.
